# 🌍 GeoLandslide AI

## AI-Based Early Warning and Landslide Risk Monitoring System for the North Eastern Region of India

GeoLandslide AI is an intelligent landslide monitoring and early warning system designed to support landslide risk assessment across the North Eastern Region (NER) of India.

The system combines environmental parameters, sensor-based monitoring, geographic visualization, and AI/ML-based risk assessment to identify potentially dangerous conditions and provide timely warnings.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

**AI-Based Early Warning and Landslide Risk Monitoring System in the North Eastern Region of India**

The North Eastern Region of India is highly vulnerable to landslides due to heavy rainfall, steep slopes, geological conditions, soil instability, and changing environmental factors.

Traditional monitoring approaches may not provide timely and easily accessible risk information.

GeoLandslide AI aims to provide a centralized platform for monitoring environmental conditions, assessing landslide risk, visualizing vulnerable zones, and supporting early warning decisions.

---

GeoLandslide AI provides an interactive monitoring platform that brings together:

## 🧠 AI/ML Approach

The system considers important landslide-related parameters such as:

- 🌧️ Rainfall intensity
- 💧 Soil moisture / soil condition
- ⛰️ Slope characteristics
- 📍 Geographic location
- 📚 Historical landslide information

These parameters can be combined to estimate the landslide risk level of a monitored zone.

The resulting risk assessment can be represented as:

**Low → Moderate → High → Critical**

This enables early identification of potentially hazardous conditions.

---

## 📡 Sensor Monitoring

Environmental sensors can provide continuous information about conditions that influence landslide susceptibility.

Important parameters include:

| Parameter | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Rainfall | Detects heavy or prolonged rainfall |
| Soil Moisture | Indicates increasing soil saturation |
| Slope | Represents terrain susceptibility |
| Location | Identifies the monitored geographic zone |
| Historical Data | Helps understand previous landslide patterns |

The current web demonstration uses simulated sensor values to showcase the complete monitoring and alert workflow.

---

## 🗺️ GIS-Based Risk Visualization

The system uses geographic visualization to represent monitored zones and their corresponding risk levels.

This allows users to:

- Identify high-risk zones
- Compare different locations
- View geographic distribution of risk
- Monitor multiple regions
- Support faster decision-making
